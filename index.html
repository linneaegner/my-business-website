who we are?

we are a running group that loves pop music, fika and glitter. our team unites people from different professions. during our post-run coffee you can hang out with artists, programmers or athletes. there's no chance of boredom with us!

we meet every saturday morning, rain or shine. pace doesn't matter — glitter and good vibes do. after the run we always grab fika together and swap playlist tips.

new runners are welcome anytime. just show up, say hi, and we'll make sure you feel at home from kilometre one.

how to join us?

join us! all you need to do is arrive at one of our saturday integration sessions. we will explain how our group works, what events are about to happen and you'll also get a chance to know us on a personal level. free coffee included :)
